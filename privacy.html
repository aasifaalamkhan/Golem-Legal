Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Golem respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Golem mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your basic account profile (name, email address, and profile photo) via Firebase Authentication. We also store your credit balance, purchase receipts, and generated media history.

2. Uploaded Media & Prompts

Images and text prompts you submit are transmitted securely to our backend inference providers (such as fal.ai) solely to fulfill your generation request. We do not sell your personal data or uploaded media.

3. How We Use Information

We use your information to authenticate your account, process credit purchases and subscriptions via Google Play Billing, deliver your creations, enforce content safety, prevent abuse, and improve app reliability.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your account data, wallet ledger, and creations are stored securely in Google Cloud / Firebase with strict per-user access controls.

5. Deleting Your Data

You can delete individual creations anytime from the Creations tab, or permanently delete your entire account and associated data in-app via Account → Delete Account. See our Account & Data Deletion page for details.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, email mail.imotion.ai@gmail.com.

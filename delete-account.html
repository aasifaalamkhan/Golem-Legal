Account & Data Deletion

App: Golem (com.golem.ai)

Option 1: Delete Directly Inside the App (Immediate)

  1. Open the Golem app and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile avatar (top right) to open Account.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm deletion. Your account and data are permanently deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request Deletion by Email

If you no longer have access to the app, send an email from the Google account address associated with your Golem account to mail.imotion.ai@gmail.com with the subject "Golem Account Deletion Request". Requests are processed within 7 business days.

What Data Is Deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account (email, display name, profile photo)
  • Your user profile document and credit wallet balance
  • All generated images, videos, and prompt history stored in your account
  • Your credit usage and transaction records

Subscriptions Note

Deleting your Golem account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play or App Store subscription. Please cancel any active subscription in your Google Play / App Store subscription settings.
